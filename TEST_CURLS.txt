====================================================
 CURLS (una linea — compatibles con Postman e n8n)
====================================================


--- 0. HEALTH CHECK ---

curl http://127.0.0.1:8585/health


--- 1. LISTAR perfiles de DICloak ---

curl http://127.0.0.1:8585/profiles


--- 2. ABRIR perfil ---

curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8585/profiles/open -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"name\": \"#5 Chat Gpt Pro\", \"timeout\": 90}"


--- 3. VER perfiles corriendo y sus puertos ---

curl http://127.0.0.1:8585/profiles/running


--- 4. EXTRAER y guardar cookies del perfil abierto ---

  OPCION A — perfil abierto via API (nombre se detecta solo):
curl -X POST "http://127.0.0.1:8585/cookies/extract?port=55915"

  OPCION B — perfil abierto manualmente en DICloak (mandar el nombre):
curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8585/cookies/extract -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"port\": 55915, \"name\": \"#5 Chat Gpt Pro\"}"

  >> port  = debug_port del perfil (ver paso 3)
  >> name  = nombre exacto del perfil en DICloak (solo si no lo detecta solo)
  >> Guarda en: cache\cookies\#5_Chat_Gpt_Pro.json


--- 5. VER todos los caches guardados ---

curl http://127.0.0.1:8585/cookies/


--- 6. INYECTAR sesion en perfil destino ---

curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8585/cookies/inject -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"port\": 55915, \"file\": \"#5_Chat_Gpt_Pro.json\"}"

  >> port  = debug_port del perfil que va a RECIBIR la sesion
  >> file  = nombre exacto del .json en cache\cookies\
